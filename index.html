Privacy Policy — Cattle Feed Cost Calculator

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Cattle Feed Cost Calculator is developed by F-Tech Developers (contact: mianfeezan420@gmail.com). This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

Information stored on your device

All calculators, ingredient prices, saved calculations, reports and settings are stored only on your device (local database). We do not operate a server for this app, and your calculation data is never sent to us or to any third party by the app itself.

No account, no registration

The app works fully offline and does not require any login, account, or registration. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location through the app.

Backup and sharing features

If you use the Backup/Export feature, a data file is created only when you tap export, and it goes only where you choose to send or save it (for example your own files or a messaging app). If you use Share, the calculation summary is shared only through the app you select. We do not receive a copy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google may collect and use a device advertising identifier and other device information to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising technologies page.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on Android).

Data safety summary (for Google Play)

  • Data collected by the app itself: None.
  • Data shared by the app itself: None.
  • Third-party SDK: Google AdMob may collect device/advertising IDs for advertising and analytics, under Google's policies linked above.
  • All locally stored app data can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. The app also provides a "Clear history" option in Settings.

Children

The app is a general-purpose calculator and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
